**Stock Control** counts stock down when an order is paid, and stops customers adding more than is in stock.

## The stock list

Open **Stock**. Each product shows how many are **In stock**, its **Reorder at** level, what is **On order**, and its status.

## Change a product's stock

Use **Set stock** on the product's row.

- **Units on the shelf** is how many you have.
- **Reorder at (optional)** is the level at or below which the product shows up for reordering. Leave it blank for no alert.
- **On order (units)** and **Expected date** record stock ordered from your supplier and not yet received. This is for your information and does not change the status or what customers see.
- **Note (optional)** stays with the product.

Use **Receive stock** when a delivery arrives, **History** to see every change, and **Stop tracking** to stop counting a product.

## Statuses

Under **Settings**, choose an **In stock status** (one that allows buying) and an **Out of stock status** (one that does not). When a tracked product runs out it moves to the out-of-stock status. When stock arrives it moves back, but only if it is still on the out-of-stock status, so a pre-order or discontinued product is never overwritten.

## If an order was short

An order's page shows when stock ran out before it was paid, and by how much. **Return items to stock** puts back what was taken, once. Only use it if the goods are coming back.
